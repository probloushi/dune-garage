Dune Garage privacy policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
Developer: Ahmed Al Balushi
Scope: Dune Garage for iOS and iPadOS, offline version 1.0.0 and its public TestFlight beta.

Information handled by the game

This version does not send personal information or gameplay data to the developer's servers. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics services. No account or sign-in is required. Online multiplayer and its server connection are disabled in this build.

Local progress and settings

The game stores campaign progress, garage and vehicle choices, the business name you enter, and settings on your device to save and resume play. These records are not uploaded by the game. There is no in-game cloud save or cross-device synchronization in this version. Deleting the app removes its local data; operating-system device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Support you choose to send

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the message or attachments you send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to address the request and related issues, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion by emailing pixltech_info@gmail.com.

TestFlight and Apple services

Apple operates TestFlight and automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback and screenshots you submit through TestFlight are also shared. The developer uses this information to investigate issues and improve the game, keeps it only as needed to resolve those issues, and does not share it with third parties. Apple handles this information under its own terms and privacy practices. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy information for details.

Sharing and tracking

The developer does not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking. Support emails are handled by the email service provider, and beta distribution and feedback are handled by Apple. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

This support website

This website uses no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or contact forms added by the developer. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and technical request logs, to deliver and protect the site. External links and email services operate under their own privacy practices.

Children

The game does not request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact the developer to request deletion.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current offline version. If a future version adds online services or changes data handling, the policy and app disclosures will be updated before those features are made available.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Ahmed Al Balushi at pixltech_info@gmail.com.
