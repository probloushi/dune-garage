Help with Dune Garage for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email pixltech_info@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, game version and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots help; please avoid including personal information.

TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Your first drive

  1. Open the game and tap Continue.
  2. Choose a starter vehicle and confirm your choice.
  3. Use the on-screen steering wheel and pedals to drive. Follow the amber destination marker and use the interaction button to meet Nasser.
  4. Open the garage board for story progress, jobs and your vehicles.

Performance and controls

If driving feels slow, open Settings and choose the Performance graphics setting. Settings also contains touch-control adjustments and the English/Arabic language selection. Allow the initial scene time to load on first launch.

Saving and resuming

Progress is saved locally after committed actions. Reopen the game and tap Continue to resume. This beta has no cloud save or device-to-device sync. Deleting the app deletes its local progress. Starting a new game can replace your current campaign, so check the in-game confirmation before continuing.

Online play and purchases

This beta is single-player and works offline. Online multiplayer is disabled. No account, real-money purchases, subscriptions or advertisements are required.

Beta availability

The public TestFlight invitation needs an approved build before installation is available. Apple manages TestFlight availability and build expiration.

Privacy requests

Read the privacy policy for details about local progress, support messages and TestFlight feedback. You can contact the same email address with a privacy question or request.
